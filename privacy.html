1. Who we are

This website belongs to Shital Foods (brand: SF Soda), Near Cinema Talkies Chowk, Market Road, Ward No. 8, Nagbhid, Dist. Chandrapur, Maharashtra — 441205. FSSAI Lic. No.: 21525060003112. In this policy, "we" means Shital Foods.

2. What information we collect

We collect only what you choose to type into our forms:

  • Contact form: name, phone / WhatsApp number, email (optional), subject and your message.
  • Member / Dealer registration: name, mobile number, village / city, email (optional); for dealers also shop / firm name, GSTIN (optional) and FSSAI number (optional).
  • Orders and offers: the message you send us on WhatsApp, such as items, quantity and offer code.

We do not collect passwords, OTPs, bank account, UPI PIN, debit / credit card details or Aadhaar / PAN through this website.

3. How your information reaches us

This website has no server database of its own. When you press the send button, your details are placed in a WhatsApp or SMS message on your own phone, and the message reaches us only when you press Send there. WhatsApp and your mobile operator handle that message under their own privacy terms.

4. Why we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and take or deliver your order.
  • To review and process your member or dealer / distributor request.
  • To tell you about offers, prices and new flavours, only if you have registered as a member or dealer.
  • To meet legal, tax and food-safety requirements where applicable.

We use your information only for these purposes and for nothing else without asking you first.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We may share it only with (a) our delivery or distribution partners, to the extent needed to complete your order, and (b) government authorities when the law requires.

6. Storage and security

Messages and registration details are kept on the phone / WhatsApp account of Shital Foods. We keep them only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, and then delete them. No method of electronic communication is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive personal or financial details to us.

7. Cookies and third-party services

Our website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Your shopping cart stays in your browser only. To work, the page loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to Google Maps and WhatsApp; those services may receive your IP address and device details under their own policies. Our website host may also keep technical server logs.

8. Your rights and choices

You may ask us at any time to: tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop contacting you. You may withdraw your consent whenever you wish. Send us a WhatsApp message on +91 97640 26807 and we will act on it within a reasonable time.

9. Children

Member and dealer registration is meant for adults aged 18 and above. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to register.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.

11. Contact / grievance

For any question or complaint about your information: Chandan W. Kose (Proprietor), Shital Foods, Nagbhid — Phone / WhatsApp: +91 97640 26807.
